A watchlist that feels like yours.

HerWatchlist is a student startup prototype for discovering Korean dramas, Chinese dramas, and Western movies in one place. Instead of endlessly scrolling, users can explore a curated 200-title catalog or ask Hera, the entertainment-focused AI assistant, for mood-based suggestions.

Our principles

  • ♡ No links to pirated streaming services.
  • ✦ Spoiler-conscious summaries and clear warnings when spoiler discussion is requested.
  • ♡ Honest, balanced descriptions rather than misleading paid promotion.
  • ✦ Source-aware catalog information; the 200-title starter catalog is manually curated for demonstration.

Built with

HTML5 · Tailwind CSS · JavaScript · Gemini & Groq AI

HerWatchlist is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any film or streaming studio.
